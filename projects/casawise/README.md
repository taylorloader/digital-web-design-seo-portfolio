# Casawise — Full Website Rebuild & Rebrand

## Project status

**In progress.** A full rebuild and rebrand of the Casawise website, an Edmonton company offering both property management and realty services.

## The brief

Bring property management and realty together under one refreshed brand and one website, so owners, tenants and buyers each find a clear path to the service they need.

The brand needs to communicate two things at once:

- **Edmonton as a vibrant, engaging city** worth living and investing in.
- **A trustworthy, full-service property management company** that handles everything with care and respect.

## My role

- **Rebrand:** brand direction, messaging and visual identity for the new site.
- **Website rebuild:** site structure, page design and build, rebuilt from the ground up.
- **Content & copy:** original website copy organized around the two service lines.
- **Photography library:** curating and building an Edmonton photo library in Canva to give the site a local, lived-in feel.
- **SEO:** on-page optimization and search-ready structure for local property management and realty searches.
