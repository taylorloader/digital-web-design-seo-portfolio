# Best Western Plus Rose Country Hotel

> Best Western Plus Rose Country Hotel is a full-service, pet-friendly hotel in Camrose, Alberta, Canada, operated by Denham Hospitality. It has 112 guest rooms, an indoor pool with a waterslide, the Camrose Resort Casino on site, Rosie's Kitchen restaurant, Joker's Den lounge, and the Regal Ballroom for weddings, meetings and events.

- Address: 3201 48 Avenue, Camrose, Alberta, T4V 0K9, Canada

- Amenities: indoor pool with waterslide, hot tub, 24-hour fitness centre, free Wi-Fi, free parking (including truck, RV and motor coach), cold-weather plug-ins, guest laundry, room service, on-site casino, pet friendly (up to two dogs in designated rooms), 100% smoke-free
- Operator: Denham Hospitality, which also operates Best Western Premier Denham Inn & Suites and Best Western Plus Edmonton Airport Hotel in Leduc
