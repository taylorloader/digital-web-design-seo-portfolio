# Best Western Premier Denham Inn & Suites

> Best Western Premier Denham Inn & Suites is a full-service hotel in Leduc, Alberta, Canada, located minutes from Edmonton International Airport (YEG). It offers guest rooms and suites, a complimentary 24-hour airport shuttle, two on-site dining venues, and facilities for weddings, meetings and group stays.

- Address: 5207 50 Avenue, Leduc, Alberta, T9E 6V3, Canada
