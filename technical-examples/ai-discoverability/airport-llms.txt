# Best Western Plus Edmonton Airport Hotel

> Best Western Plus Edmonton Airport Hotel is a full-service, locally owned hotel in Leduc, Alberta, Canada, about an 8-minute drive from Edmonton International Airport (YEG). It offers 116 guest rooms and suites, a complimentary 24/7 airport shuttle, Park, Sleep & Fly stays, free hot breakfast, an indoor pool with a waterslide, and Barney's Pub & Grill on site.

- Address: 5705 50 Street, Leduc, Alberta, T9E 6Z8, Canada
- Telephone: +1-780-986-6550
- Email: plusguestservices@denhamhotels.com
- Check-in: 4:00 PM
- Check-out: 11:00 AM
- Amenities: complimentary 24/7 YEG airport shuttle, free hot breakfast, indoor pool with waterslide, hot tub, fitness centre, guest laundry, business centre, free parking (including truck, RV and motor coach), EV charging, 100% smoke-free
- Operator: Denham Hospitality, which also operates Best Western Premier Denham Inn & Suites in Leduc
