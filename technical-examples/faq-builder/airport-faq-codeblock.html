Planning Your Stay

Where is Best Western Plus Edmonton Airport Hotel located?
We're at 5705 50 Street, Leduc, Alberta T9E 6Z8, just off Highway 2 and minutes from Edmonton International Airport (YEG) and Nisku Business Park.
How far is the hotel from Edmonton International Airport?
About an 8-minute drive. Our complimentary airport shuttle runs 24/7.
How far is the hotel from Edmonton attractions?
West Edmonton Mall, the University of Alberta and Rogers Place are each about 30 to 35 minutes away by car.
What makes the Plus different from other hotels near YEG?
Full-service amenities: an indoor pool with a waterslide, a hot tub, a fitness centre, free hot breakfast, a 24/7 airport shuttle, and Barney's Pub & Grill on site, home to Canada's first Pour-Your-Own Beer Wall. Add flexible meeting and banquet space, all minutes from Edmonton International Airport.
Is the hotel locally owned?
Yes. We're part of Denham Hospitality, a family of locally owned and operated hotels in Leduc that includes our sister property, Best Western Premier Denham Inn & Suites.
What does “Our business is hospitality” mean?
Every guest interaction, from the front desk to housekeeping to the breakfast buffet, is designed to make travellers feel welcomed and cared for.
Do you support the local Leduc community?
Yes. As part of the Denham Hospitality family of locally operated hotels, we proudly support local Leduc events, sports teams and community partners.
How can I contact the hotel?
Call (780) 986-6550, email plusguestservices@denhamhotels.com, or use our contact page. The front desk is open 24 hours, and we usually reply to emails within one business day.

Arrival & Airport Shuttle

Do you offer a complimentary airport shuttle?
Yes. Our complimentary shuttle runs 24/7 to and from Edmonton International Airport (YEG). Call the front desk at (780) 986-6550 when you land, or to schedule a pickup.
Can the shuttle handle a large group arriving together?
Yes. The shuttle is available to every guest, including group blocks. For large groups arriving at the same time, we can schedule pickups in advance.
Do you offer Park & Fly?
Yes. Our Park, Sleep & Go rates let you stay the night before your flight, leave your vehicle with us while you travel, and take our 24/7 shuttle to and from YEG. See Packages & Specials or call (780) 986-6550 for current rates and parking terms.
Is there a convenience store or vending on site?
Yes. We have a sundry shop and ice and vending machines.
Can I store my luggage?
Yes. Luggage storage is available at the front desk.

Reservations & Rates

What time is check-in and check-out?
Check-in is at 4:00 p.m. and check-out is at 11:00 a.m.
Can I check in early or check out late?
Early check-in and late check-out are available on request, subject to availability. Fees may apply.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellation policies vary by rate and package. Your policy is shown in the rate rules when you book and in your confirmation email.
What is the minimum age to check in?
Guests must be at least 18 years old, with valid government-issued photo ID and a credit card, to check in.
Can I combine packages with Best Western Rewards points?
Member rates and points-earning are available on most packages, though some promotional rates are excluded. Confirm when you book.
How do I book a package?
Select the package in the booking widget, or call the front desk at (780) 986-6550.
Are packages refundable?
Cancellation policies vary by package. See the rate rules when you book.
Can I customize a package or add an enhancement?
Yes. You can add extras such as charcuterie boards, chocolate-covered strawberries or a bottle of wine when you book, or by contacting the front desk in advance.

Rooms & In-Room Experience

How many rooms does the hotel have?
116 guest rooms and suites.
What's in the guest rooms?
Pillow-top mattresses and premium linens, blackout drapes, individual climate control and air-conditioning, a mini-fridge, a microwave, a Keurig coffee maker, a 55" smart TV with streaming services, a work desk, a hairdryer, and an iron and ironing board. Wi-Fi is free.
What suites do you offer?
Our suites have fireplaces, whirlpools and kitchenettes, including the Whirlpool Suite (1 King) and the Kitchenette Suite (1 King). They suit longer stays and special occasions. See Rooms & Suites.
What is the maximum occupancy per room?
King rooms and king suites sleep 2. Queen rooms sleep 4.
Are rollaway beds and cribs available?
Yes. Rollaway beds are available on request for $20 per night, and cribs are available on request.
Are connecting rooms available?
A limited number of connecting rooms are available on request, subject to availability. Call the front desk at (780) 986-6550 to arrange.
Can I request a specific room or floor?
Yes. We accept requests but can't guarantee them; they're subject to availability.
Is the hotel smoke-free?
Yes. The entire hotel is 100% smoke-free.

Families & Pets

Is the hotel family-friendly?
Yes. Children 12 and under stay free in their parents' room with existing bedding, and the indoor waterslide is a family favourite.
Are pets allowed?
No. The hotel is not pet friendly. Service animals are welcome.

Accessibility & Special Requests

Do you have accessible rooms?
Yes. We have two barrier-free rooms, available on request. Features include extra-wide bathroom doors, walk-in or roll-in showers with safety bars, a bathtub seat, a raised toilet with grab bars, lowered vanities, closed-captioned TV, and visual alarms with emergency alert lighting.
Is there an elevator?
Yes. A wheelchair-accessible elevator serves all floors, and all rooms open onto interior corridors.

Connectivity & Technology

Is Wi-Fi free?
Yes. Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel, in every guest room and in the meeting rooms.
Do you have a business centre?
Yes. Our on-site business centre has a computer, a copier and a fax.
Can I stream on the room TV?
Yes. Rooms have 55" smart TVs with streaming services and an HDMI port.

Parking & On-Site Access

Is parking free?
Yes. Outdoor parking is free for hotel guests and for meeting attendees who aren't staying overnight.
Can I park a truck, RV or bus?
Yes. We have parking for trucks, RVs and motor coaches, which is useful for sports teams and work crews.
Do you have EV charging?
Yes. One complimentary NACS (Tesla-style) EV charger is in the entrance parking lot. It's available 24/7 on a first-come, first-served basis.
Do you have block heater plug-ins?
Yes. Plug-ins are available for winter parking.

Amenities & Services

Is breakfast included?
Yes. A complimentary hot buffet breakfast is included with every stay, served daily from 6:00 to 10:00 a.m. It rotates eggs and meats, potatoes, French toast or pancakes, oatmeal, yogurt parfaits, fresh fruit, breads and pastries, with gluten-friendly options.
Do you have a pool, hot tub and waterslide?
Yes. Our heated indoor pool has a waterslide, and there's a hot tub. Both are open year-round from 8:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Children must be supervised by an adult.
Do you have a fitness centre?
Yes. Our complimentary fitness centre is open from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. It has treadmills, a stair stepper, a stationary bike, a universal gym and free weights.
Is there guest laundry?
Yes. Self-serve laundry is available for a small fee from 8:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Valet laundry and dry cleaning are also available.
Is the front desk open 24 hours?
Yes. Wake-up calls are also available.

Dining: Barney's Pub & Grill

Is there a restaurant on site?
Yes. Barney's Pub & Grill is inside the hotel. It's home to Canada's first Pour-Your-Own Beer Wall, with 12 rotating Alberta craft beer taps, scratch-made pub food, daily specials, big screens for the game, and a glass-enclosed patio with a fireplace that seats 40.
What are Barney's hours?
Monday and Tuesday 10:00 a.m. to 1:00 a.m., Wednesday to Saturday 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 a.m., and Sunday 10:00 a.m. to 1:00 a.m.
Is there room service?
Yes. Room service is available daily from 11:00 a.m. to midnight.
Can I order takeout from Barney's?
Yes. Order online or call (780) 986-6550.
Does Barney's have daily specials?
Yes, including Taco Tuesday, Wine & Pasta Wednesday, and late-night happy hour daily from 9:00 p.m. to midnight.
Can I reserve a table at Barney's?
Yes. Reservations are available through Barney's website.

Weddings, Meetings & Events

Do you have meeting and event space?
Yes. Our event spaces suit everything from boardroom meetings to receptions:
  • Explorer Ballroom (1,900 sq ft; divides into Explorer A and B, 950 sq ft each): up to 120 for receptions or theatre seating, and up to 112 for banquets
  • Denham Room (870 sq ft): up to 60 for receptions, 55 theatre-style and 48 for banquets
  • Barney's Office boardroom: up to 20
  • Lobby Lounge: receptions for up to 45
On-site catering, bar service and in-house audio-visual support are available.
What AV and event services do you offer?
Projectors, screens, wireless microphones and sound equipment, all supported by our in-house team, plus Wi-Fi in every meeting room, bar service and beverage packages, and on-site catering. A permanent boardroom setup is available, and some rooms have natural light.
What catering options are available?
Chef-crafted buffets, plated dinners, hors d'oeuvres, interactive food stations and late-night snacks, with menus for casual gatherings through to elegant receptions.
Can I host a larger event or wedding?
Yes. For events of up to 200 seated guests, our sister hotel in downtown Leduc, Best Western Premier Denham Inn & Suites, has the Discovery Ballroom (up to 200) and the Marquis Ballroom (up to 90). Our sales team plans events at both hotels. See meetings and events and weddings.
How do I request an event proposal?
Email our sales team at sales@denhamhotels.com or call (780) 986-6550.

Group Bookings & Sports Teams

What's the minimum number of rooms for a group rate?
Group rates start at 10 rooms per night. Courtesy room blocks of under 20 rooms don't require a deposit. Terms are set out in a simple room block agreement covering dates, room types and the cut-off deadline. Learn about group room blocks.
How far in advance should I book a group block?
We recommend booking 60 to 90 days ahead for weddings and conferences, and as early as possible during peak season (May to September). We accept last-minute blocks, subject to availability.
Can guests book individually?
Yes. Each block gets its own booking link or rooming list, so guests can book and pay individually.
Do you offer welcome bags or in-room amenities for groups?
Yes. Our sales team can coordinate these, and fees apply for custom welcome bags.
What if my group needs fewer rooms than booked?
Unbooked rooms are released back to the hotel on the cut-off date in your room block agreement. Contact our sales team before the cut-off if you need to adjust your block.
Do you host sports teams and tournaments?
Yes. We regularly host teams, with group rates, free bus and trailer parking, a 24/7 airport shuttle, and quick access to Leduc and Nisku sports venues.

What is there to do near the hotel?
Leduc's Main Street (a filming location for several productions), the 8 km Telford Lake Boardwalk, Stone Barn Garden, the Leduc Recreation Centre, the Maclab Centre for the Performing Arts, and Rig Hand Distillery. Ask the front desk about distillery tours.
Is the hotel close to Nisku Business Park?
Yes, just minutes away, with direct Highway 2 access. See our location.
